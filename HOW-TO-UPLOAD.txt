Pájaro y Bestia — upload to your ISP
====================================

THIS ZIP INCLUDES assets/styles-*.css. If the site looks unstyled,
the assets folder did not upload.

1. Empty public_html completely.
2. Unzip this zip.
3. Upload EVERYTHING inside, especially the assets folder.
4. In File Manager, open public_html/assets/ and confirm you see
   styles-Bh5puArx.css
5. Site must be at the ROOT of public_html, not a subfolder.
6. If .htaccess is hidden, upload htaccess and rename it to .htaccess.
7. Point the domain. Turn on HTTPS.

Do not upload the old zip. Replace everything.
